    HOW TO PLAY

    • Move the Chomper onto a number and chomp it. Only numbers matching the rule count — clear them all to finish the level.
    • Chomp a wrong number, or let a Gobbler catch you, and you lose a Chomper. You start with 3.
    • Scoring: +10 per correct chomp, streaks build a bonus, and clearing a level pays 100 + 10 per level.
